WHY THIS CORPUS
                The official NIST ACVP sigVer set (already exercised by
                test/ACVP_MLDSA44.t.sol) does not systematically cover
                malformed HintBitPack encodings.  Wycheproof does:
                tcId 15..19 and 137..139 are exactly the "non-canonical hint
                encoding" class — reverse order, repeated index, non-zero
                padding, limit going backwards, limits that read past the hint
                section.  tcId 18 ("signature with a repeated hint") is the
                Wycheproof case that reproduces CVE-2026-24850 in RustCrypto
                `ml-dsa`; it passes ACVP and fails Wycheproof.
