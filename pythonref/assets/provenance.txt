WHAT IT IS NOT  **NOT a NIST artefact, and it carries NO NIST authority.**
                This is the single most important line in this file, because
                the FILENAME says "PQCsignKAT" and the FORMAT is NIST's, and
                neither makes the CONTENT a NIST answer.

                * The SEEDS and MESSAGES are the NIST KAT harness's: they are
                  the output of AES-256-CTR-DRBG seeded with bytes(range(48)),
                  48 seed bytes then `mlen` message bytes per record.  That is
                  MACHINE-CHECKED — tools/fixtures/kat_build.py replays the
                  DRBG and asserts every record's `seed` and `msg` byte for
                  byte, and aborts the fixture build otherwise.
                * The KEYS and SIGNATURES are NOT NIST's.  They were generated
                  in-tree by pythonref/dilithium_py under **FIPS 204
                  ML-DSA-44**.  Also machine-checked: kat_build.py asserts the
                  pythonref oracle accepts all 100 as pure ML-DSA-44 with an
                  EMPTY context before emitting any of them as must-accept.

                Concretely: the same-named file in upstream dilithium-py
                (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GiacomoPope/dilithium-py/
                main/assets/PQCsignKAT_Dilithium2.rsp, sha256
                38ed991c5ca11e39ab23945ca37af89e059d16c5474bf8ba96b15cb4e948af2a)
                holds ROUND-3 Dilithium2 vectors.  Its `seed`/`mlen`/`msg`
                fields agree with this file record for record; its `pk`, `sk`
                and `sm` DO NOT.  Round-3 Dilithium2 and FIPS 204 ML-DSA-44 are
                different signature schemes, so that difference is expected and
                is the reason this file cannot be replaced by the upstream one.

UPSTREAM TOOL   https://github.com/GiacomoPope/dilithium-py  (MIT), whose
